// what it does

The notes you would take if you weren't busy talking.

Start a session and keep working. Carrow listens, sorts what it hears, and keeps the list current while you talk.

capture

Notes as you go

Decisions, action items, blockers, ideas and open questions, sorted while the conversation is still happening.

follow-through

A backlog with owners

Action items land as cards with an owner and a due date when one was mentioned, so nothing depends on someone's memory.

report

A read on the session

When you stop, Carrow writes up where people agreed, where they talked past each other, and what to sort out next.

solo

Think out loud

Talk through a plan on your own, the way you would with a producer, and get back the plan, the risks and the next steps.

team

One team feed

See what your team discussed and decided without another status meeting. Sessions are grouped by topic.

sources

Works with what you use

Your computer's mic and audio, your phone, or a wireless mic. It also connects to Slack, Discord, Teams, your calendar, email and Git.

// your data

You choose where it goes.

Every session follows the same path. At each step you can see where the data is going and change it.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Recording starts when you start a session, on your computer or phone.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    On your own machine, so audio never leaves it. Or in the cloud, through Carrow or with your own key.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    With your own AI key, or a model running on your own hardware.

  4. 04

    Share

    Sessions start personal. Team work goes to your team feed, and you can keep any session to yourself.

Consent first

Everyone in the session should know it's being recorded. Carrow asks you to acknowledge that before you can record.

Locked to your team

Each team's data stays with that team. No team can see another team's sessions.

Your keys, your models

Use your own AI provider key, or run a local model so analysis stays on hardware you control.

Not for surveillance

Carrow is for teams working together. It can't be used to monitor people without their knowledge, or by law enforcement or government agencies.
