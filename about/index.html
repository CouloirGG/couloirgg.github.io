// track record

What I've done.

I earn trust between teams that don't agree, fix the process with them, prove the change with data, build the tool when one is needed, and hand it off so it runs without me. Plenty of people are good at one or two of these. I do all five.

  1. 01Earn trust
  2. 02Fix the process
  3. 03Prove it with data
  4. 04Build the tool
  5. 05Hand it off
partnership

Turn friction into partnership

I've joined groups where two teams argued more than they agreed. By showing each side how the other could help, I rebuilt the relationship until the partner team started handing over work they used to keep to themselves.

decisions

Meetings that end in a decision

I've rebuilt recurring triage and launch meetings that had drifted into tangents: a set time per item, the right people in the room, a call before moving on. Then I stepped out and they kept running.

expectations

Clear service levels

I've turned unspoken expectations into simple tiers, like bronze, silver and gold, so anyone requesting work knows exactly what they are asking for and how long it will take.

follow-through

Problems that don't repeat

I've taken a thin post-incident review process and built it into a measure leadership relies on, with training, templates, feedback channels and clear owners. Then I handed it to the people closest to the work.

data

The pattern nobody was tracking

I've dug back through years of history to find a recurring failure, brought it to the teams who could fix it, and watched it nearly disappear. I build the dashboards and tell the story at the level each audience needs.

cost

Cheaper and better

I've led evaluations of paid vendors against tools a company already had, and supported a provider migration on a tight timeline that came out cheaper and performed better.

structure

More capacity, same headcount

I've reorganized teams around automation and clear focus areas so they could take on new responsibilities without new hires or longer hours.

people

People who grow

My teams have consistently rated their management among the strongest in the organization, and several people I've led have earned promotions. I give people room to lead, and I've brought new team members in across countries and time zones.

building

I build the tool too

I design and build AI tools end to end, including ones that pull chat, tickets and build data into a single view of what is at risk and who owns it. I also write the guidance teams follow to use AI safely.
