Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy describes how CouloirGG LLC handles information from this website and enquiries.

Website analytics

This site uses Plausible Analytics to understand page visits and selected link clicks. Plausible uses cookie-free analytics and presents aggregate reports. Its data policy explains what it processes and retains.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to these services when loading pages, which exposes connection information such as your IP address to those providers.

Email and enquiries

When you email us, we receive your address and the information in your message so we can respond. Please only include information needed for your enquiry.

If you previously used the commission enquiry form, your submission was processed by Formspree. Its privacy policy explains how it handles submissions. Contact us with questions about information previously submitted.

External links

Linked websites have their own privacy policies. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

Questions and data requests

Contact hello@couloir.gg with questions about this policy or information you have shared with us.
