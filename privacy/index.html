Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

CouloirGG LLC ("we", "us") is committed to respecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect (very little) and how we handle it.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics service. Plausible does not collect personal data, does not use cookies, and is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. All data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual visitors.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. No first-party cookies, no third-party cookies, no tracking pixels.

Email contact

If you contact us via email at hello@couloir.gg, we will receive and store your message and email address for the purpose of responding. We do not add contacts to any mailing list or share email addresses with third parties.

AXIS Producer

AXIS Producer is a local-first application. All audio processing, transcription, and analysis happens on your device. No data leaves your machine. No telemetry is collected. No usage data is sent to CouloirGG or any third party.

LAMA and ARC View

LAMA and ARC View are open-source tools licensed under GPLv3. They do not collect any user data, do not phone home, and do not include analytics or telemetry of any kind.

Third-party links

This site contains links to external websites (GitHub, Discord, YouTube, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@couloir.gg.
