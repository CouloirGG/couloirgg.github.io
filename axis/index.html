AXIS Producer

What AXIS Does

AXIS Producer is a desktop productivity tool that records your team's work sessions and uses AI to extract what matters: decisions made, action items committed, blockers identified, and open questions raised.

After each session, AXIS generates a deep analysis report. Not just what was said, but how the team communicated: where they agreed, where they talked past each other, and what to do differently next time.

Features

Session Recording

Capture mic audio, system audio, and text input from meetings, design sessions, and working conversations.

AI Extraction

Decisions, action items, blockers, ideas, and questions, automatically categorized from your conversations.

Session Reports

Deep post-session analysis: communication patterns, points of agreement and tension, actionable recommendations.

Team Sync

Share session intelligence across your team. See what everyone is working on without asking.

Local-First

Audio and transcripts stay on your machine. Cloud sync is opt-in and only shares structured summaries.

Multi-Source

Captures from voice, Claude Code, clipboard, Git, Slack, email, and calendar, all in one timeline.

Privacy & Consent

AXIS Producer is a productivity tool, not surveillance software. Privacy and consent are built into the product at every level.

Consent Required

All users must acknowledge a consent notice during setup. Recording only starts when the user explicitly initiates a session.

Self-Attestation

Users attest during signup that the tool will be used for lawful internal team communication only, not for surveillance or monitoring non-consenting parties.

Local by Default

Audio recordings and full transcripts never leave the user's machine. Only structured summaries are synced to the cloud when the user opts in.

No Government Use

AXIS Producer explicitly prohibits use by government agencies, law enforcement, or for monitoring individuals without their knowledge and consent.

How It Works

  • Start a session: click the tray icon or press Ctrl+Shift+F9
  • Work normally: AXIS captures audio, text, and activity in the background
  • Get structured notes: every 5 minutes, AI extracts decisions, actions, blockers
  • Session report: when you stop, get a deep analysis of the entire session
  • Team briefing: see what your whole team discussed, clustered by topic

Privacy Policy

AXIS Producer ("the Service") is operated by CouloirGG ("we", "us").

  • Data collected: Audio recordings, transcripts, and structured session notes are stored locally on the user's device. When cloud sync is enabled, only structured summaries (decisions, action items, blockers) are transmitted to our servers.
  • Audio recordings: Never uploaded to our servers. Processed locally using on-device AI (Whisper) or transmitted to the user's chosen cloud provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq) for transcription only when the user explicitly configures this.
  • Third-party services: When using cloud features, structured summaries are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL). LLM analysis uses Anthropic's Claude API. Payment processing uses Stripe.
  • Data retention: Local data is retained until the user deletes it. Cloud-synced summaries are retained for the duration of the subscription.
  • Data deletion: Users can delete their account and all associated data at any time via the app settings.
  • Consent: All users must acknowledge a consent notice during onboarding. The tool records only when the user explicitly starts a session.
  • Prohibited uses: Surveillance of non-consenting parties, law enforcement monitoring, government use, and any use that violates applicable wiretapping or recording consent laws.
